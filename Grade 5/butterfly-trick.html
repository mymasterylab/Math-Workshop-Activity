🦋 Butterfly Magic

A quick trick for telling which fraction is bigger — without a number line, without a calculator, without guessing.

  1. 1Multiply diagonally. Left wing: 2 × 4 = 8. Write it above the left fraction.
  2. 2Other diagonal. Right wing: 3 × 6 = 18. Write it above the right fraction.
  3. 3Compare the wings: 8 < 18, so 2/6 < 3/4. The bigger wing sits over the bigger fraction.

You'll get 5 questions, easy to tricky. Every question makes you draw both wings before you can answer — that's how the magic sticks.
